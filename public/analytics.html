Methodological Footnote · Definitions of Legal Gates (G1, G2, G3, G4) & Ranking System
1. Multi-Level Ranking Hierarchy Circles (#1 to #14) are ranked statewide first by Composite Score % (Weighted Clearance: 45% Draft Remarks, 30% Final Orders, 15% GA Auth, 10% Intake), then by Real Files Moved. Units with zero movement are placed at the bottom sorted by active caseload. Divisions (#N.1 to #N.D) and Officer Desks (#N.D.1 to #N.D.U) are similarly ranked within their respective parent circle/division.
2. Definitions of the 4 Legal Gates (G1, G2, G3, G4) • Gate 1 (G1 · 10% Weight): Intake Assignment — Files moved out of No Action Taken into LCO Proposal Stage. Clearance rate g1 = G1 / Opening No-Action Stock.
• Gate 2 (G2 · 15% Weight): Counsel Sanction — Files moved out of LCO Proposal to Government Advocate (GA) Authorization. Clearance rate g2 = G2 / Opening LCO Stock.
• Gate 3 (G3 · 45% Weight): Parawise Remarks & Defense — Files completed with Parawise Remarks (PWR) and advanced into active Court Hearing. Clearance rate g3 = G3 / Opening Draft Remarks Stock.
• Gate 4 (G4 · 30% Weight): Final Order Compliance & Closed — Reduction in pending final court orders through executed compliance or appeal proposal. Clearance rate g4 = G4 / Opening Final Orders Stock.
Composite Score % = (0.10·g1 + 0.15·g2 + 0.45·g3 + 0.30·g4) × 100
3. Full Multi-Stream Case Coverage Tracks all 8 CCMS legal streams: High Court: Writ Petitions (WP), Special KSAT (S-KSAT), Civil Contempt (CCC), Writ Appeals (WA); KSAT: Original Applications (OA), Contempt (CA), Misc Applications (MA), Review Applications (RA).
